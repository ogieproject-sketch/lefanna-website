PLUS Services

Corporate Division

Excellence in Luxury Hospitality Advisory

PLUS (Lefanna Leisure Unlimited Services) is our premier corporate advisory and resort management division. We provide full-scope strategic advisory services, spatial concept development, yield management, and operational leadership to boutique resort developers and private estate owners across Bali and Indonesia.

01 / ADVISORY

Pre-Opening Advisory & Concept Development

We guide luxury hospitality developments from initial vision to grand opening. Our scope encompasses market feasibility studies, architectural spatial alignment, luxury branding guidelines, software PMS integration, and strategic pre-sales marketing launches.

02 / OPERATIONS

Operational Management Systems

Delivering flawless daily resort operations. We establish rigorous standard operating procedures (SOP), fine-dining kitchen hygiene protocols, front-desk guest relations workflows, housekeeping standards, and eco-conscious vendor supply chain logistics.

03 / DISTRIBUTION

Sales & Revenue Management

Driving global visibility and maximizing RevPAR. We engineer targeted digital advertising campaigns, automated yield revenue management, multi-OTA channel manager connectivity, corporate contracting, and global travel agent representation.

04 / HUMAN CAPITAL

Human Resources & Service Training

Nurturing exceptional talent rooted in genuine warmth. We recruit top-tier hospitality professionals, conduct customer service excellence workshops, and train operational teams in authentic Balinese hospitality philosophies and luxury standards.
