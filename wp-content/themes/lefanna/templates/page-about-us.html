Our Vision & Mission

The L.E.F.A.N.N.A Philosophy

LEFANNA is built upon a philosophy that reflects intentional hospitality, meaningful connection, and refined living. Every word within LEFANNA represents the values that shape the way experiences are created, felt, and remembered.

L

A quiet radiance that comes from warmth, authenticity, and experiences that naturally leave a lasting impression.

An understated sense of beauty expressed through thoughtful details, timeless design, and refined simplicity.

The seamless harmony between space, service, and experience, creating stays that feel effortless and intuitive.

Management Fee Structure

Property Category Management Fee Basis Calculation
Homestay / Guest House 10% From Gross Operating Profit (GOP)
Private Villa / Luxury Villa 12% From Gross Operating Profit (GOP)
Hotel / Boutique Resort 14% From Gross Operating Profit (GOP)

Marketing & Advertising Service

Service Charge Coverage
Digital Marketing & OTA Optimization 3% From Room ARR (Average Room Rate)

Marketing Scope Includes:

  • OTA management and optimization
  • Social media management
  • Online advertising campaigns
  • Promotion strategy and campaign planning
  • Basic content coordination
  • Market positioning support
  • Revenue and pricing strategy support

Notes & Terms:

  • Management fee is calculated based on monthly Gross Operating Profit (GOP).
  • Marketing fee is calculated separately from room ARR revenue.
  • Advertising budget spending is excluded and billed separately based on campaign requirements.
  • Custom operational structures and service scopes may be adjusted depending on property size and operational complexity.
  • Long-term management partnerships may receive customized commercial arrangements.
